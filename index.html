Who I am

A data student who likes real, local data.

I started in robotics as a lead programmer, building a system that waters crops and one that watches a patient's vital signs. That taught me to care about whether something actually works.

Now I study Data Science and Analytics at GCTU and bring the same attitude to data. My favourite project so far uses Ghana's inflation figures, taken straight from the Ghana Statistical Service. I am building toward a career as an AI engineer, so I am learning machine learning and language models next.
